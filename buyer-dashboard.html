REQUEST WORKSPACE

LC request details

A formal LC request can only be submitted against an existing VTG order. Banks must review and authorize issuance; this form does not issue an LC.

How an LC request progresses

01
Prepare purchase evidence

Approved quotation or pro-forma invoice, supplier details and agreed terms.

02
Bank review & due diligence

Your bank reviews the applicant, facility, transaction and applicable requirements.

03
Draft LC terms

Parties review amount, dates, documents, shipment terms and presentation conditions.

04
Issuance & shipment documents

Only the issuing bank can confirm issuance. Documents are checked against the final LC terms.
