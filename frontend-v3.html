About Vintage Trade Global

Vintage Trade Global (VTG) is a digital trade platform built to connect African businesses with trusted suppliers, financial institutions, logistics providers, inspection agents and international trade opportunities across China, South Korea and the wider global market.

VTG brings key parts of international trade into one connected ecosystem — from supplier discovery and product sourcing to business verification, inspection, trade documentation, payments, logistics and trade intelligence.

Our goal is to make cross-border trade more transparent, accessible, organized and connected, particularly for African businesses seeking reliable international suppliers and commercial opportunities.
