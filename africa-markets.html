Pay safely

Choose the right way to pay

The right payment route depends on where your supplier is and how much trust has been built.

Paying suppliers in China or Korea

For first orders and larger values, use a bank-backed method rather than paying the full amount up front.

  • Letter of credit, with payment released against agreed documents
  • Bank transfer in stages, tied to inspection
  • Keep the proforma invoice and Form M aligned
See VTG finance options

Paying inside Africa

PAPSS lets participating banks settle African trade payments in local currency, without routing through the US dollar. It reports 28 countries and 190+ banks and fintechs.

  • Availability depends on your bank and your counterparty’s country
  • Reported as not yet fully operational for the BEAC (CEMAC) region
  • A BCEAO pilot is scheduled, not started
Read the report

Check before you release money

Most import losses come from paying before the goods are verified. Inspect the product and the supplier first.

  • Verify the supplier and the product
  • Inspect at the factory or before loading
  • Release payment against documents and photos
