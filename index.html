Letter of Credit (LC)
SWIFT MT700 · BANK-GUARANTEED · SAFEST METHOD

An LC is the gold standard for international trade. Your bank guarantees payment to the supplier's bank only after compliant shipping documents are presented. Zero counterparty risk.

Buyer requests LC on VTG
Choose order, amount, and expiry. VTG forwards to your bank (GTBank/Zenith).
Bank issues SWIFT MT700
Your bank ring-fences funds and sends LC to supplier's bank in China.
Supplier ships & presents docs
Bill of Lading, Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin.
Bank verifies & releases payment
SWIFT MT103 sent to Bank of China on compliant docs. Supplier paid.
Quick Panel
Quick LC Request
Best for: Orders above $10,000 · New supplier relationships · High-value machinery, vehicles, bulk goods
Telegraphic Transfer (T/T Wire)
SWIFT · 1–3 BUSINESS DAYS · TRUSTED SUPPLIERS
T/T is the most common payment method for established trade relationships. Typically 30% deposit upfront and 70% before or after shipment. Always verify supplier bank details via official channels before wiring.
Agree payment terms with supplier
30/70 split is standard. Negotiate based on relationship and order size.
Submit Form M to your bank
CBN-mandated for all imports above $10,000. VTG manages this for you.
Bank processes SWIFT wire
Funds sent via SWIFT MT103 to supplier's bank. Arrives in 1–3 days.
Supplier ships on payment confirmation
Shipping documents sent to VTG and buyer on dispatch.

Documentary Collection (D/P)
DOCUMENTS AGAINST PAYMENT · BANK-INTERMEDIATED
D/P (Documents against Payment) is a middle-ground between T/T and LC. The supplier's bank sends shipping documents to your bank — you only receive them after paying. Lower bank fees than LC but less protection.
Supplier ships & hands docs to their bank
Bank of China (or ICBC) holds original Bill of Lading and shipping docs.
Collecting bank sends to your bank
Documents forwarded to GTBank/Zenith with instruction: release only against payment.
You pay your bank
Once payment is made to GTBank, they release original shipping documents to you.
You collect cargo at port
Present original B/L at Tin Can Island Port. FGR handles customs clearance.
