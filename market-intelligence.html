Practical trade playbooks

Make the next step clearer.

Use these checklists to organise your thinking. For shipment-specific rules, verify the current requirements with the relevant authority or licensed professional.

Cost planning

Build a landed-cost comparison

Compare supplier offers on the same basis: product cost, origin transport, freight, insurance, duties and taxes, port and clearing charges, financing, inspection and final delivery. Record the currency, assumptions and quote validity.

Trade statistics reference
Logistics planning

Map the shipment, not just the route

Confirm the booking cut-off, route and transshipment, estimated transit, document deadlines, arrival handling, customs readiness and last-mile plan. Reconfirm schedules with your carrier or logistics provider.

Compliance readiness

Verify rules before dispatch

Confirm the correct product classification, destination requirements, applicable permits and standards, labelling, inspection and document sequence. Keep a dated record of the official guidance used.

Supplier due diligence

Assess supplier reliability before paying

Match the legal business identity to documents, validate product specifications, request samples or inspection evidence, agree milestones in writing and use appropriate independent verification for higher-risk orders.
