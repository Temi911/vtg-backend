A practical trade sequence

Use these steps as a guide; actual availability depends on your account and connected services.

STEP 01Define the requirement

Product, specification, quantity, destination and target delivery date.

STEP 02Assess the supplier

Check evidence and agree inspection requirements before committing.

STEP 03Plan landed cost

Account for goods, freight, insurance, duties, port and local charges.

STEP 04Prepare delivery

Confirm documents, shipment milestones and customs arrangements.
