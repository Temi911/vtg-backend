What it brings together

Keep the important details connected.

A trade can involve several organizations, records and decisions. Workspace is intended to keep those related details linked to the same transaction.

Orders & terms

Keep product details, quantities, agreed pricing, currency, delivery terms and order changes organized.

Trade documents

Collect the documents relevant to a transaction and show whether each item is uploaded, under review or needs attention.

Verification & inspection

Connect verification requests, inspection evidence, findings and recorded approvals to the relevant trade.

Payment milestones

Organize amounts due, payment references and recorded status. Actual payment processing depends on the relevant authorized provider.

Logistics & Trade Atlas

Link shipment references, route information, ports, vessel milestones, customs progress and destination updates where available.

Trade communication

Keep transaction-related conversations and decisions associated with the appropriate trade and authorized participants.

From start to finish

Understand the status without chasing every detail.

A clear timeline helps participants understand completed steps, pending actions and issues that may affect progress.

A connected trade timeline

  • Trade enquiry and commercial terms recorded
  • Order confirmation and required documents
  • Verification, inspection and approval milestones
  • Payment status and shipment preparation
  • Shipment movement, customs and delivery updates
  • Completion record and issue history

When something needs attention

  • Flag missing or inconsistent documents.
  • Record quantity, quality or shipment discrepancies.
  • Assign follow-up to the responsible participant.
  • Keep evidence and decisions attached to the issue.
  • Preserve a history of updates and resolution.
Built around roles

The right information for the right participant.

For buyers

  • Review agreed product and order information.
  • Access relevant documents and inspection evidence.
  • Follow recorded payment and shipping milestones.
  • Raise questions or discrepancies through the relevant process.

For suppliers and service partners

  • Share relevant order updates and supporting records.
  • Respond to authorized requests and discrepancies.
  • Coordinate assigned inspection or logistics milestones.
  • Keep responsibilities and updates linked to the trade.
Access is permission-based. Trade information can be sensitive. Private transaction records should only be available to the relevant authorized participants, subject to account permissions and applicable policies.
Clear expectations

A connected workspace supports the process—it does not replace accountable providers.

What Workspace is for

  • Organizing trade records and milestones.
  • Connecting relevant VTG tools and service workflows.
  • Showing recorded updates, evidence and outstanding actions.
  • Helping participants coordinate with clearer context.

What it does not automatically guarantee

  • That a supplier, product or document is genuine merely because it appears in a record.
  • That a payment has settled unless confirmed by the relevant provider.
  • That shipment tracking or ETA data is live or complete in every case.
  • That customs clearance, regulatory approval or delivery is guaranteed.
Frequently asked

Trade Workspace, explained.

Is Trade Workspace a public information page?

No. This page explains the service. Actual trade records belong in the private workspace and should be available only to authorized participants.

How is it different from Trade Guide?

Trade Guide explains trading concepts and practical preparation. Trade Workspace is intended to help participants manage records, updates and milestones for a particular transaction.

Will the workspace show my shipment on Trade Atlas?

Where the relevant shipment and tracking data are connected and available, the workspace is designed to link the trade to its logistics journey in Trade Atlas.

Can every participant see every document?

No. Access should follow role-based permissions and the participants authorized for that transaction.

Does VTG directly hold or release every payment?

No such assumption should be made. Payment processing, custody and settlement depend on the authorized financial institution or payment provider involved.
