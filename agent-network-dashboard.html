Lead handling standards

Recommended controls before a live rollout

1. Record the source.
Capture how the introduction arrived and who is responsible for the next step.

2. Confirm consent.
Only record business contact information when there is a valid reason and appropriate permission.

3. Protect supplier relationships.
Use referral ownership, non-circumvention terms and clear commission agreements approved by VTG.

4. Never promise a commission.
Payout terms must be agreed in writing and confirmed against a verified completed transaction.

This page does not calculate or promise commissions. Any future amount must come from an authorised backend ledger and the applicable signed agreement.

Proposed commission lifecycle

1 · Terms agreed
Rate, eligible event, currency, exclusions and payout timing documented in writing.

2 · Trade reference linked
Referral is linked to a unique trade or order reference with access limited by role.

3 · Milestone verified
Authorised staff confirm the agreed trigger; disputes remain reviewable.

4 · Finance approval
Approved ledger entry, tax/compliance checks and payment status are recorded.

Controls needed before launch

• Written commission and referral agreement

• Unique trade reference and duplicate-referral checks

• Approval trail for adjustments and disputes

• Role-restricted financial data

• Currency, tax and payout status handled by finance

• No editable client-side commission balances
