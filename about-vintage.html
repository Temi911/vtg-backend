About Vintage Trade Global

One connected ecosystem for global trade.

Vintage Trade Global (VTG) is being built to connect commerce, sourcing, verification, finance, currency intelligence, logistics, documentation and trade intelligence across Africa, China, South Korea and global markets.

What VTG is

More than a marketplace.

International trade often requires buyers and suppliers to move between separate systems for sourcing, verification, payment, banking, shipping, customs, documentation and communication. VTG is designed as a connected environment where these activities can work together around the same trade journey.

Marketplace & sourcing

Find trade opportunities with more context.

VTG brings product discovery, supplier profiles, buyer enquiries, supplier communication and trade activity into the wider transaction environment.

Supplier verification can include business information, documentation, licences and other checks appropriate to the relationship. Verification status should remain visible and evidence-based rather than implying that every supplier is automatically verified.

Where a buyer requests physical checks, VTG's agent workflow can support pre-shipment inspection, product evidence, photographs and other verification records.

Currency flexibility

Trade across currencies with greater visibility.

Cross-border transactions can involve a buyer's local currency, a supplier's preferred currency and a separate settlement currency. VTG's financial vision is to make those relationships easier to understand and coordinate.

Multi-currency trade

Support trade workflows involving currencies such as NGN, CNY/RMB, USD, EUR, GBP, KRW and other supported currencies as the platform expands.

FX intelligence

Exchange-rate information, comparisons, conversion tools and currency intelligence can help users understand how rates affect trade costs.

Settlement coordination

Where supported by participating financial partners, VTG can connect the commercial transaction with the appropriate payment and settlement workflow.

Buyer currency → Trade currency → Settlement currency

A Nigerian buyer may price or budget in NGN, negotiate with a supplier in CNY or USD, and settle through an appropriate financial institution. VTG is designed to make the relationship between these stages clearer.

Forex & market intelligence

Understand the financial environment around a trade.

Currency movement can change the economics of an international purchase. VTG's intelligence layer is designed to put currency information closer to sourcing, pricing and trade decisions.

Rate information

View relevant exchange-rate information and compare currencies when evaluating a transaction.

Trade impact

Understand how currency changes can affect product costs, settlement requirements and landed trade economics.

Financial clarity

Know the currency side of the transaction.

VTG can bring currency information, trade calculations and financial workflow context closer to the point where commercial decisions are made.

Information and tools do not constitute investment or financial advice. Actual FX execution and regulated financial services depend on authorised providers and applicable law.

Digital assets

Preparing for new forms of cross-border settlement.

VTG's longer-term financial vision includes digital-asset and cryptocurrency-related trade infrastructure where legally permitted and where appropriately regulated partners are available.

Digital settlement

Explore compliant ways digital assets and stablecoins may support faster cross-border settlement in jurisdictions and workflows where permitted.

Blockchain records

Consider tamper-resistant transaction and document records where blockchain technology provides genuine value.

Responsible access

Digital-asset services are subject to jurisdiction, regulation, licensing and partner availability. VTG will not present crypto as universally available or risk-free.

Bank integration

Putting financial institutions inside the trade journey.

Banks are a core part of international commerce. VTG is designed to connect eligible trade workflows with participating financial institutions instead of treating banking as a separate conversation.

02Trade information & documents

Trade finance

Support workflows around instruments such as letters of credit, documentary collections, buyer or supplier finance and other appropriate facilities.

Payment coordination

Connect commercial orders with payment confirmation, settlement information and required documentation through participating providers.

Bank workspace

Provide participating institutions with a structured environment for relevant trade-finance enquiries, documentation and transaction status.

VTG does not itself turn an unregulated website feature into a banking service. Regulated financial products and transactions are provided by appropriately authorised institutions and partners.

Logistics & shipping

Connect the order to its physical journey.

Once a transaction moves from commerce to movement, VTG is designed to connect shipment information with the trade record.

That can include freight, ports, vessels, air freight, customs milestones, shipping documents, route information, shipment status and delivery coordination.

Example: Guangzhou → Nansha → Vessel → Tin Can Island → Lagos → Customs → Buyer.

Documents & verification

Keep the evidence connected to the transaction.

Trade documents

Commercial invoices, packing lists, shipping records, certificates, customs documents and other transaction records can be associated with the relevant workflow.

Inspection agents

Approved agents can support product or supplier checks, factory visits, photographs, video evidence and pre-shipment verification where requested.

Traceable status

VTG AI

Intelligence that sits alongside the trade.

VTG AI is designed to help users navigate trade information and workflows, including sourcing questions, logistics context, market information, documents and general platform assistance.

AI can help explain complexity, but it does not replace a customs authority, bank, lawyer, accountant, licensed financial professional or other regulated institution.

Market intelligence

Information for better decisions.

VTG's intelligence layer can bring together relevant trade news, currency information, shipping developments, market movements and regional trade developments.

The aim is to put useful context closer to the transaction rather than forcing users to search across disconnected sources.

Trust & responsibility

Built for serious international commerce.

VTG's ecosystem is designed around clear information, responsible trade and appropriate controls.

Verification

Use evidence-based verification and clearly distinguish verified information from information still under review.

Responsible finance

Work with appropriately authorised financial institutions and providers for regulated financial services.

Trade integrity

Support responsible business practices, documentation, complaint resolution and the VTG Code of Conduct.

Data & privacy

Handle user and transaction information according to applicable privacy, security and data-protection requirements.

Compliance

Where applicable, trade and financial workflows may involve identity checks, AML controls, sanctions screening and other legal requirements.

Human oversight

Technology supports the process; qualified people and authorised institutions remain responsible for decisions that require professional or regulatory authority.
