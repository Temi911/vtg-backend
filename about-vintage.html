About Vintage Trade Global

Built to connect global trade.

Vintage Trade Global (VTG) is a digital trade platform connecting African businesses with trusted suppliers, financial institutions, logistics providers, inspection agents and international trade opportunities.

VTG brings supplier discovery, product sourcing, business verification, inspection, trade documentation, payments, logistics and trade intelligence into one connected ecosystem.

Our goal is to make cross-border trade more transparent, accessible, organized and connected for businesses participating in international commerce.
