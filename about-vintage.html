About Vintage Trade Global

One connected ecosystem for global trade.

Vintage Trade Global (VTG) is being built to connect commerce, sourcing, verification, finance, currency intelligence, logistics, documentation and trade intelligence across Africa, China, South Korea and global markets.

VTG is being built around one long-term vision: to create a connected international trade environment where Commerce + Finance + Logistics + Intelligence + Technology work together instead of operating as disconnected pieces.

The vision is to make international trade more connected, transparent, accessible and organised for Africa, China, South Korea and the wider world. A buyer should be able to move from discovering a supplier to verifying the opportunity, arranging appropriate finance or payment, tracking the shipment, understanding the logistics and customs journey, and reaching delivery without losing the context of the original transaction.

For suppliers, VTG aims to provide a structured way to present products, business information, verification evidence and trade opportunities. For financial institutions and authorised providers, it aims to connect relevant trade information with appropriate financing, payment and settlement workflows. For logistics participants, it aims to connect orders with ports, vessels, routes, customs milestones and delivery. For every participant, the goal is a clearer trade journey built around reliable information, evidence and responsible coordination.

VTG's purpose is therefore not simply to be another marketplace or another logistics tool. It is to bring the important participants and stages of a cross-border transaction closer together, creating an ecosystem where technology helps people understand what is happening, what comes next and what information is needed.
