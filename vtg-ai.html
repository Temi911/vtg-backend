How to read AI answers

Verified recordUse only when a connected, authorised source confirms the fact.
Estimate or guidanceUseful for planning, but confirm before making a commercial decision.
Needs confirmationMissing or unverified inputs should be resolved before acting.

VTG AI supports decisions; it does not independently approve suppliers, guarantee a shipment position, set customs duties, authorise finance or make payments. Those actions require verified records, the proper VTG workflow and human confirmation.
