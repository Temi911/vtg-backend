What VTG AI helps you do

It helps organise information and prepare next steps. It does not replace official checks, licensed professionals or independent supplier verification.

Sourcing & RFQs

Turn a product idea into a structured request with specifications, quantity, destination, packaging, target timing and questions for suppliers.

Quote comparison

Compare offers on a consistent basis while clearly marking missing prices, unclear Incoterms, unconfirmed freight and quote validity.

Inspection planning

Prepare checklists, acceptance criteria and photo-evidence requests for product quality and cargo readiness before dispatch.

Trade documents

Organise typical document requirements and flag which details must be confirmed with customs, banks or destination authorities.

Logistics handoffs

Map origin, port, carrier, customs and delivery milestones without presenting an estimate as a confirmed carrier event.

Risk & evidence gaps

Identify unanswered questions, distinguish claims from evidence and highlight decisions that need independent confirmation.

Example: preparing a buyer RFQ

Before asking suppliers to quote, VTG AI would help collect the key details that make offers comparable.

  • Product specification, grade/model and required certifications
  • Target quantity, packaging, destination and requested delivery date
  • Preferred Incoterm, quote currency and quotation validity
  • Questions to resolve before comparing supplier offers

Next step: confirm the product details and destination so the RFQ is specific enough to price.

Sample only. Any named requirements, prices, routes, duties or supplier claims must be independently confirmed before a real transaction.
