Vintage Trade Global · Responsible Trade

Code of Conduct

VTG connects buyers, suppliers, financial institutions, service partners and trade professionals. Everyone using the platform is expected to act honestly, respectfully and responsibly throughout trade-related activity.

1. Honest identity and information

  • Provide accurate personal, business, product and transaction information.
  • Do not impersonate people or organisations or submit forged, misleading or unauthorised documents.
  • Correct information promptly when a material detail changes.

2. Respectful communication

  • Communicate professionally and respectfully with other users, VTG staff and service partners.
  • Do not threaten, harass, discriminate against, intimidate or deliberately mislead another person.
  • Use messaging and enquiry tools for legitimate trade-related purposes.

3. Lawful and responsible trade

  • Use VTG services only for lawful commercial activity and comply with applicable trade, customs, sanctions, export-control and product-safety requirements.
  • Do not use the platform to promote prohibited goods, evade legal obligations, facilitate fraud or misrepresent the origin, quality or status of goods.
  • Confirm applicable import, export, licensing, tax and documentation requirements with relevant authorities and qualified professionals.

4. Fair dealing and transaction integrity

  • Describe products, prices, quantities, delivery expectations and material conditions accurately.
  • Respect agreed transaction terms and promptly communicate material delays, disputes or changes.
  • Do not manipulate enquiries, reviews, offers, verification records or other platform activity.

5. Privacy, security and confidential information

  • Protect account credentials and do not access another user’s account or data without permission.
  • Share personal, business, financial and shipment information only when there is a legitimate reason and appropriate authorisation.
  • Report suspected unauthorised access, fraud or misuse through VTG’s contact channels.

6. Verification and service limitations

Provide genuine supporting documents when verification is requested. A profile, document or service status displayed by VTG should not be treated as a guarantee that a transaction, counterparty, payment, inspection or shipment will succeed. Carry out proportionate checks before making commitments.

7. Reporting and response

Concerns about suspected misconduct can be raised through the VTG Contact page or by emailing vintageafricatrade@gmail.com. VTG may review reports and restrict or suspend access where appropriate, subject to its applicable platform terms and processes.

This page summarises expected platform conduct. It should be read together with the VTG Signup & Platform Agreement and Privacy Policy. It does not replace legal advice or applicable law.
