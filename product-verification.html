A clear process

From request to inspection report.

VTG helps coordinate the service between the buyer and an assigned agent. The scope, evidence and next steps should be clear before the inspection begins.

STEP 01

Share your requirements

Provide the product, supplier location, quantity and the details that matter to your order.

STEP 02

Review the fee

Calculate the estimated fee from the available options before creating your request.

STEP 03

Agent checks the goods

After payment is confirmed and an agent is assigned, the agreed checks and evidence can be collected.

STEP 04

Review the findings

Use the available report and evidence to inform your next decision about the order.

Inspection scope

Checks that follow your order.

Inspection scope depends on the service level selected and what can reasonably be checked at the supplier’s location. You can describe your priorities in the request.

Product model and specifications
Quantity and visible condition
Packaging and labels
Accessories and agreed details
Visible damage or defects
Photo or optional video evidence
Be specific in your instructions. A visual inspection does not automatically certify hidden components, laboratory performance, regulatory compliance or authenticity unless those checks are explicitly arranged and evidenced.
Evidence you can review

Clear findings, not empty promises.

Evidence depends on the inspection carried out and the report submitted. Only completed and recorded checks should be presented as verified.

Inspection photos

Visual records of the goods and agreed checkpoints, where captured by the assigned agent.

Checklist and findings

A record of the requested checks and the observations actually made during inspection.

Order-linked report

Inspection details associated with the relevant request so the buyer can review the available results.

VTG inspection is an additional decision-support service, not a guarantee against fraud, concealed defects or later damage. Results reflect the agreed scope and conditions at the time of inspection.
