The opportunity

Make storage part of the trade network.

VTG can connect trade demand with physical infrastructure across key markets and corridors. Warehouse and storage partners can explore opportunities around bonded facilities, consolidation and deconsolidation, inventory holding, fulfilment, port-adjacent storage and distribution support.

Why it matters

From supplier to buyer, without losing the inventory layer.

Storage is a critical bridge between manufacturing, shipment movement, customs and final delivery. Over time, eligible facilities can become discoverable infrastructure within the VTG Trade Atlas, helping users understand where goods can be stored, consolidated, inspected or prepared for the next movement.
