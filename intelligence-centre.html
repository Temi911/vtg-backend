Market & product explorer

Search international merchandise trade statistics from within VTG. Choose a reporting market, import/export flow, year and HS product code. Results are returned from the connected UN Comtrade public preview service when available.

Choose your market and search. Results will include the reporting year and source status.

Trade statistics may be incomplete, revised or delayed. A missing result does not mean there was no trade. Figures are not a real-time quote or forecast; source and reporting period are shown with returned records.
