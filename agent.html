The role

Be the trusted local connection behind global trade.

A VTG Trade Agent supports buyers when they need someone on the ground to inspect products, verify supplier readiness, document what was seen and coordinate the next step.

01

Product inspection

Visit a supplier or location and inspect goods against the assignment provided by VTG.

02

Photo & evidence

Capture clear, genuine photographs and observations for the buyer's review.

03

Supplier coordination

Communicate professionally with suppliers and coordinate information needed before shipment.

04

Shipment readiness

Help confirm assigned goods are ready for the next stage of the trade journey.
